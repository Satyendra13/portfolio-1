## Slide 5: How AI Tokens Work During a Phone Call
During a phone call, AI prices change based on how long the call lasts:
- **How Fast People Talk**: An average person speaks about 150 words per minute (~200 tokens per minute).
- **The Conversation History Grows**: On every back-and-forth exchange, the AI must re-read what was said earlier plus the company rules to stay on track.
  - In a 3-minute call, the AI processes between **6,000 and 10,000 input tokens**.
- **AI Answers Are Kept Short**: The AI gives clear, spoken replies of 50 to 70 words (~400 to 600 output tokens for a 3-minute call).
- **Cost by Model**:
  - **Fast & Cheap (Gemini 2.5 Flash / GPT-4o mini)**: Costs only **$0.003 to $0.008 per minute**. Perfect for routine questions, appointments, and order lookups.
  - **Heavy Models (GPT-4o / Claude 3.5)**: Costs **$0.020 to $0.040 per minute**. Used for complex sales or difficult arguments.

## Slide 8: The Hybrid Model (AI + Human Teamwork)
We do not replace human workers; we pair them together for the best results:

- **Tier 1: AI Handles 70% to 80% of Calls**:
  - Repetitive, simple questions (order status, refund rules, hours, simple bookings).
  - Checks customer ID and collects basic info.
  - 24/7 night support and holiday rush support.
- **Tier 2: Humans Handle 20% to 30% of Calls**:
  - Complicated problems, angry callers, and emotional situations.
  - Big sales negotiations and VIP accounts.

**The Step-by-Step Flow**:
1. Caller calls in $\rightarrow$ AI answers immediately on the first ring.
2. AI solves the simple question or collects account details.
3. If the problem is too hard or the caller gets upset, the AI transfers the call in under 2 seconds.
4. The human agent sees a pop-up window with the customer's name, problem, and call transcript. The human finishes the call smoothly.

## Slide 9: How a Call Works (Simple Workflow)
1. **Customer Clicks "Call Support"**: Audio connects instantly over the web browser using WebRTC (LiveKit).
2. **AI Greets the Customer**: The AI speaks a friendly welcome in English & Hindi.
3. **Customer Talks**: The AI listens, cuts background noise, and converts speech to text.
4. **AI Checks the Company Knowledge Base**:
   - Matches the customer's question with official company files.
   - If it's a private action (like checking an order), it asks for 2-step verification (Order ID + last 4 phone digits).
5. **AI Speaks the Answer**: Converts text to a natural voice in under 1.5 seconds.
6. **Customer Can Interrupt Anytime**: If the customer starts speaking while the AI is talking, the AI goes silent in under 300 milliseconds.
7. **Warm Transfer (When Needed)**: If needed, a human agent joins the same room. The customer never has to re-explain their issue.

## Slide 10: System Architecture & Tech Stack (Simple Explanation)
We built the system using proven, modern tools:

- **FastAPI (Python 3.12)**: The main brain API. Fast, handles thousands of users at once, and connects to databases cleanly.
- **LiveKit (WebRTC Audio)**: Runs the audio room. Lets the customer, AI, and human agent talk in the exact same room with ultra-low delay.
- **Silero VAD (Voice Activity Detection)**: Detects when the user starts and stops talking so the AI knows when to listen and when to reply.
- **ElevenLabs Scribe & Voice**: Best-in-class real-time speech recognition and lifelike voices that sound like a real person.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash**: Lightning-fast reasoning AI. Understands caller intent and crafts short, clear answers in fractions of a second.
- **LangGraph**: State machine that controls conversation rules. Makes sure the AI follows company steps and never gets stuck in a loop.
- **PostgreSQL 16 with pgvector**: Stores customer records, call transcripts, and vector search embeddings in one secure database with strict company privacy.
- **React + Tailwind CSS**: Clean web screens for the customer calling page, human agent softphone, and supervisor analytics dashboard.
