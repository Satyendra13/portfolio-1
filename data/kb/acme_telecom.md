# Acme Telecom Customer Knowledge Base

## 1. Mobile & Fiber Plans
Acme Telecom offers three primary service tiers:
- **Starter Mobile Plan**: $30 per month. Includes 10 GB 5G data, unlimited talk and text within North America.
- **Unlimited Ultra Mobile Plan**: $60 per month. Includes unlimited 5G Ultra Wideband data, 25 GB hotspot, and international texting to 50 countries.
- **Fiber Home Broadband 1000**: $70 per month. Includes 1 Gbps symmetrical upload and download speeds with no data caps and a free Wi-Fi 6 router.

## 2. Billing & Payment Policies
- Bills are generated on the 1st of every month and due on the 20th.
- A late fee of $15 is applied if payment is not received within 5 days of the due date.
- Auto-pay discount: Customers enrolling in automatic bank payments receive a $5 monthly bill credit.

## 3. SIM Replacement & Upgrade Policy
- SIM card replacements cost $10 at retail stores or are shipped free of charge within 3 business days.
- Device upgrades are eligible after 12 consecutive months of active payments.

## 4. Technical Troubleshooting & Support
- Slower internet speeds can often be resolved by restarting the fiber optical network terminal (ONT) for 30 seconds.
- APN settings for Acme Mobile data: APN Name `acme.net`, MMSC `http://mms.acme.net`.

## 5. Cancellation & Refunds
- Services can be canceled at any time with 30 days advance notice.
- Equipment must be returned within 14 days of cancellation to avoid a $150 unreturned router fee.
