# QuickCart E-Commerce Knowledge Base

## 1. Shipping & Delivery
- **Standard Shipping**: Free on orders over $50. Delivered within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Express Shipping**: $12 flat rate. Delivered within 1 to 2 business days.
- Orders placed before 2 PM EST are dispatched on the same business day.

## 2. Return & Refund Policy
- Items can be returned within 30 days of purchase in original packaging and unworn condition.
- Prepaid return shipping labels are provided free of charge for store credit returns; a $4.99 shipping fee is deducted for original payment method refunds.
- Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the return package reaches our warehouse.

## 3. Order Tracking & Changes
- Orders can be modified or canceled within 60 minutes of placing the order via customer support or the QuickCart app.
- Tracking numbers are emailed automatically once the shipping courier scans the package.

## 4. Damaged or Missing Items
- If an item arrives damaged or missing, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos to receive an immediate replacement or full refund.

## 5. Gift Cards & Discounts
- Promo codes cannot be stacked with clearance sale items unless explicitly stated.
- QuickCart Digital Gift Cards never expire and have no maintenance fees.
